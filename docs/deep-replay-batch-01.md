# Deep replay batch 01 — 8 worlds, era-aware history lines

Status: high-cost narrative reasoning pass over the structural shortlist. These are replay proposals and history-line extractions; canonical mutations still require World authority replay.

## Era anchors used

The replay treats dated 2026 conditions as observed only after their dates. It also carries 2027–2030 technology/economic waves as uncertain branches. Important 2026 anchors include: AI+ diffusion into science/industry/public services; graduate-employment support in advanced manufacturing, modern services and AI-related fields; expansion of AI agents into multi-step work; pressure on junior knowledge-work ladders; and October policy emphasis on avoiding technology investment bubbles while continuing industrial AI deployment.

Future branches are never treated as facts. A character can prepare for them, bet on them, or be wrong about them.

## Replay protocol

For each shortlisted world I replayed five kinds of node when available:

1. first material scarcity collision;
2. first career ambiguity;
3. first AI/workflow repricing;
4. first lifecycle exit or institutional transition;
5. a delayed consequence at least one season later.

A replay choice was retained only when it could be stated from the actor's local knowledge and had a legal path through money/time/permission/consent constraints. Trend exposition was discarded unless it changed an actual option.

## world-004 — 《欠下来的不是失败》

**Premise.** Chen Lan reaches the most severe household/cash squeeze in the shortlist. She uses bounded paid work and an administrative exception to stay afloat, but refuses to turn every rescue into a permanent identity as the person who will always absorb extra work.

**History line.**

- 2026 spring: family-care pressure consumes time. Chen uses the scarce administrative exception; Lin loses that option for the cycle. The first moral fact is uncomfortable: Chen has a legitimate need and Lin still pays part of the cost.
- 2026–27: Chen accepts a bounded paid package when cash pressure becomes real. She insists that old receivables, new work and personal help remain separate.
- 2027: AI-assisted service work gets cheaper and faster. Mingheng stops valuing raw hours as much; people who can define, verify and own outcomes gain leverage. Chen learns the new workflow because it protects her bargaining position, not because the story declares AI good.
- 2027–28: debt/hardship leaves a memory. When she is finally offered a more stable external role, she accepts only after the scope and after-hours expectations are written down.
- 2028: Lin later needs a referral. Chen helps, but cannot recreate the capacity she herself consumed earlier. Her goodness is limited and therefore credible.
- Delayed echo: the company remembers a referral that failed for capacity reasons and reopens it in a later hiring cycle. Lin receives an opportunity without Chen being able to promise the outcome.

**Truth / goodness / beauty:** high truth, high goodness, high beauty. The strongest motif is **“分开”**: money/work, help/debt, competence/availability, kindness/entitlement. The cost of goodness remains visible.

## world-007 — 《没有大事发生的三年》

**Premise.** A comparatively fortunate line. No severe cash hardship. The danger is literary: success can become flat. Replay therefore keeps ordinary accumulation instead of manufacturing catastrophe.

- Lin repeatedly narrows claims and becomes unusually good at verification as AI makes first-draft analysis cheaper.
- Chen graduates without a dramatic rescue. External employment improves her material position.
- Xu's reproducibility habit, initially low-status, becomes more valuable as AI-generated analyses proliferate and institutions need people who can audit provenance.
- 2027–29: better tools shorten tasks, but recommendation slots and trusted responsibility remain scarce. The scarce object changes from labor hours to **credible judgment**.
- Lin declines one superficially prestigious opportunity whose role would turn her into permanent invisible verification labor. Nothing explodes. Her life is better because she can say no.

This is the strongest **quiet-winner** history. Beauty comes from restraint rather than reversal.

## world-009 — 《风口没有替人作决定》

**Premise.** Macro technology/industry opportunity improves while academic capacity tightens.

- A public hiring wave expands technical nonacademic options. Lin learns about it through a real recruitment channel, not omniscient narration.
- She applies while keeping the academic path open. The act costs preparation time and changes what she can finish that month.
- Chen, already outside the university, becomes the only person in the group with current knowledge of AI-enabled service workflows. Status reverses: the former delayed doctoral student now possesses information the academically smoother peers lack.
- A large employer automates part of an entry workflow. The advertised headcount falls, but a smaller verification/governance role appears. Lin is not automatically qualified.
- She loses one application and later wins another because her evidence discipline transfers.

Era wave creates both destruction and opportunity. No technology deus ex machina.

## world-010 — 《会做的人越来越多》

**Premise.** One of the strongest structural histories: three cross-actor chains and no hardship collapse.

- AI agents make routine statistical implementation dramatically easier. The social value of merely producing an analysis falls.
- Gu Wei begins rewarding Xu for documenting why a result cannot be reproduced. What looked like failure in 2026 becomes a scarce audit skill.
- Lin and Chen compete indirectly for recommendation/attention capacity while the outside market begins asking for hybrid research–AI–verification skills.
- Chen takes the faster commercial path and initially earns more. Lin stays closer to research and initially appears to lose.
- By 2028–29, a firm-wide automation wave removes some routine service work. Chen survives because she learned scope negotiation and verification; colleagues whose advantage was speed alone are more exposed.
- Lin's slower path pays only if institutions actually reward accountable research. In one future branch she gets a strong role; in another she does not. Replay preserves this uncertainty.

Strong theme: **when execution becomes cheap, responsibility becomes expensive.**

## world-017 — 《年轻人的第一阶台阶》

**Premise.** Focus shifts toward Xu Hang and the loss of junior training work.

- Entry-level reproduction, cleaning and routine coding tasks are increasingly delegated to agents.
- Xu gains speed but loses repetitions through which older researchers learned judgment.
- Gu responds by assigning him failures and audits that cannot be solved by polished output alone.
- Xu resents this because peers elsewhere publish faster. He nevertheless keeps a public failure log.
- A later hiring/research opportunity explicitly values traceable verification. The thing that slowed him becomes portable capital.

Goodness is modest: Gu does not protect Xu from competition; he gives him work that actually teaches him. Beauty depends on the delayed reinterpretation of “failure.”

## world-022 — 《第二次毕业》

**Premise.** The earlier scout already showed dense structural movement. Deep replay turns Chen's graduation into only the first transition.

- Chen finishes the doctorate after repeated material and administrative pressure.
- External work initially feels like victory: income rises, autonomy improves, academic gatekeeping recedes.
- Agentic work then changes her job. Tasks she was finally paid well to perform become cheaper.
- She faces a second graduation: remain an executor whose market price is falling, or become the person who defines scope, verifies outputs, talks to clients and owns failure.
- She chooses the latter, but the transition costs evenings of retraining and one missed promotion cycle.
- Later she can hire or refer someone. Remembering her own precarity, she refuses unpaid “trial work” even when it would benefit her team. This has a real budget cost.

This is the strongest **costly-goodness + technological-wave** line.

## world-025 — 《两种上升》

**Premise.** Lin and Chen both improve, by incompatible routes.

- Chen exits academia first and gets the visible income jump.
- Lin's research path remains slower. AI reduces the prestige of routine technical virtuosity and raises the premium on problem choice, data access and verification.
- They stop being directly comparable. Chen has money and organizational leverage; Lin has greater control over what questions she works on.
- A housing/employment decision forces the comparison back into material life: one can afford more, the other can refuse more.
- Neither is declared the winner. Their friendship survives only if they stop treating each other's life as a counterfactual verdict on their own.

This is the strongest candidate for the earlier “人生赢家” question because it refuses a single scoreboard.

## world-032 — 《后来才知道那年已经变了》

**Premise.** The transformation is slow enough that characters notice it retrospectively.

- 2026: AI is still experienced mainly as a tool and source of anxiety.
- 2027: hiring descriptions quietly change; fewer roles ask only for execution, more ask for AI workflow, verification, domain ownership or client responsibility.
- 2028: the group realizes that several “normal” career ladders have been shortened. Some people advance faster; some lose the training stage they needed.
- Xu's failure/reproduction archive becomes unexpectedly useful. Chen's client-facing verification becomes defensible work. Lin must decide whether research autonomy is worth slower material accumulation.
- No single announcement marks the era change. The beauty is retrospective: ordinary decisions made under partial knowledge acquire a different meaning later.

This is the strongest **时代感** line.

# Extracted historical trunk

The four Tier-A lines suggest one larger historical structure worth preserving for later novel construction:

> **2026–2030: the price of execution falls; the price of judgment, responsibility, trust and the right to choose changes unevenly. Three young researchers leave the same small academic world by different doors. One appears to win early and must graduate a second time when her new profession is automated. One rises slowly by preserving control over what she is willing to claim. One discovers that documenting failure is a form of capital only after the world begins producing plausible answers too cheaply. Their friendship and professional network carry the costs of scarce recommendations, housing, money and attention across years.**

This trunk is not yet a novel synopsis. It is the history line that survived the first large replay because it can carry truth, costly goodness and formal recurrence without requiring the world to reward virtue.
