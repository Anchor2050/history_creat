# Candidate history selection 2035 v1

## Status
The 2030–2035 structural trunk remains frozen. This pass generates and attacks concrete lived histories only.

Eight incompatible histories were generated from the same trunk in `candidate_histories_2035_v1.py`. Every branch keeps explicit costs and refuses a final success settlement.

## The eight worlds

### W1 — reference pressure
The first replay survives only as a reference. Chen is care-bound, Lin lets mobility decay, Xu receives late formal recognition.

Use: baseline comparison.

Weakness: too many events now look like the author's preferred interpretation rather than necessary consequences.

### W2 — mobility exercised
Lin actually takes the cross-city job. She gains work refusal power but loses proximity, welfare continuity and cheap coordination with family.

Key result: exercising an option does not prove the option was good in every dimension. Mobility is a bundle exchange.

This branch is valuable because it attacks the previous path at its exact weak point: Lin's freedom should not depend on her conveniently failing to use it.

### W3 — care relief
Formal long-term care becomes materially more useful. Chen recovers enough average time to accept a promotion with a two-city arrangement.

Key result: better policy genuinely changes a life. The world does not need suffering to preserve the trunk.

This is a necessary goodness counterfactual. If the history engine only remains interesting when institutions fail, it is biased toward misery.

### W4 — recognition during incapacity
Xu's failure archive becomes institutionally useful during a period of insomnia/anxiety in which he reduces public work and does not pursue promotion.

Key result: social usefulness, recognition, income and private flourishing separate in time. Recognition is not narrative compensation.

### W5 — friendship rupture
Xu refuses an unverifiable claim. Lin eventually adopts the smaller verifiable standard, but the friendship does not recover. The good rule survives without reconciliation.

Key result: moral correctness can produce durable relational loss. The engine must not repay integrity with emotional closure.

### W6 — paid-training collapse
Chen's paid-training project dies completely under budget pressure. Three years later only a smaller program returns, and its first lesson is the failure of the original promise.

Key result: goodness may survive only as memory, not institutional continuity.

### W7 — climate-care correlated shock
A short heat/transport interruption coincides with family medical need and breaks Chen's backup-care schedule.

Key result: coordination robustness matters more than average care hours under correlated failure.

Weakness: high shock dependence. Keep it as a stress branch, not a default source of drama.

### W8 — quiet erosion
No decisive promotion, disaster or recognition scene arrives. Chen fragments annual leave into care days, Lin stops checking outside jobs without noticing when that habit changes, and Xu's checklist diffuses into a manual without a promotion or public credit.

Key result: historical change can accumulate without announcing itself as plot.

This is the strongest control against catastrophe bias, climax bias and moral-settlement bias.

## Hard-gate result
All eight candidates pass the current mechanical gates:
- chronology is consistent;
- causal references resolve to earlier branch/trunk history;
- costs are explicitly represented;
- no final victory/moral reward event is inserted.

These gates are intentionally weak. Passing them means a branch is replayable, not canonical.

## Multi-objective result
Truth, goodness and beauty were kept separate. Closure risk and shock dependence were also tracked separately. No weighted total score was computed.

Under strict Pareto dominance, the nondominated set is:

1. **W3 care relief** — strongest test of whether a kinder institutional future still produces real history;
2. **W8 quiet erosion** — strongest combination of hard plausibility, low plot engineering and long echo.

This is informative but insufficient for literary selection. Pareto dominance can eliminate a branch that performs a unique causal attack. W5, for example, is dominated numerically but is the only current branch that asks whether a good evidentiary rule can survive while the friendship dies.

## Deep-replay shortlist
Four histories therefore advance even though only one of them is on the strict Pareto frontier:

### A. W8 quiet erosion — historical control / likely backbone
Reason: if this line remains moving after scene-level replay, the project no longer depends on spectacular shocks. It offers the best test of whether the world itself can carry literature.

### B. W5 friendship rupture — goodness attack
Reason: forces a durable human cost onto an otherwise admirable standard. It is the best current defense against moral bookkeeping.

### C. W4 recognition during incapacity — merit attack
Reason: prevents the failure archive from becoming a delayed success fantasy. It also creates a strong changed-meaning echo without requiring public triumph.

### D. W2 mobility exercised — autonomy attack
Reason: directly competes with the existing Lin path. Her life must remain credible whether she moves or stays.

W3 remains the policy counterfactual control; W6 remains a failed-good-institution branch; W7 remains a correlated-shock stress test.

## Compatibility test
W8, W5 and W4 are largely compatible: the macro world can remain quiet while Lin/Xu's relationship ruptures and Xu's work diffuses during a low-capacity period. This combination does not require a new structural mechanism.

W2 is incompatible with W8's specific Lin path because she cannot both move in 2032 and quietly continue renewing the same local life. It must remain a genuine competing world rather than being folded into a single maximal-drama line.

## Next construction
Build two fully continuous rival histories rather than one optimized super-history:

- **History Alpha — quiet rupture:** use W8 as the background rhythm, splice in W5's unrepaired friendship and W4's recognition/incapacity mismatch. Keep macro shocks ordinary.
- **History Beta — exercised mobility:** use W2 as Lin's path, retain Chen/Xu under the same frozen trunk, and test whether the story remains equally strong when Lin acts rather than hesitates.

Then replay both year-by-year with local information only. Track money, time, health, housing, care, migration, status, welfare, power and trust after every consequential choice. Do not select between Alpha and Beta until both have survived counterfactual attack and ordinary-life scenes.
