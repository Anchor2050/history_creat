## Compute concentration decision

Future high-cost replay is now concentrated on **022 + 025**.

010 and 032 are not discarded. Their strongest discoveries are imported as supporting lenses:
- Xu Hang's failure/reproducibility archive becoming capital when execution gets cheap;
- historical change becoming visible retrospectively through ordinary job ads and workflows.

The project therefore stops treating the four Tier-A histories as four equally expensive universes.

## Convergence

022 and 025 are compatible enough to converge into one candidate history.

The core question becomes:

> **当执行越来越便宜，什么仍值得一个人用几年人生去换？**

Chen carries the technological/economic line: delayed graduation -> material rise -> automation reprices her new job -> second graduation into scope/verification/responsibility -> management power -> costly refusal of unpaid trial labor.

Lin carries the autonomy line: slower material accumulation -> temptation to treat independence as isolation -> rejection of invisible verification labor -> lower but more self-directed work -> ability to decide what she will not claim.

Xu carries the epistemic line: failed reproduction -> speed anxiety -> lost junior training ladder -> verification practice -> failure records become portable capital.

The era carries no omniscient 'AI revolution' narrator. It enters as changed job descriptions, tool access, headcount, task prices, housing choices, client expectations, evaluation rules and missing junior rungs.

## Five-act historical trunk

1. **2026 — 同一张桌子**: scarcity is small and local; nobody knows which habits matter.
2. **2026–27 — 第一次上升**: Chen appears to win; Lin appears slow; material inequality enters friendship.
3. **2027–28 — 第二次毕业**: agentic work cheapens execution; Chen retrains, Lin reprices autonomy, Xu keeps failures.
4. **2028–29 — 责任变贵**: plausible output is abundant; accountable verification becomes scarce.
5. **2029–30 — 两种自由**: one person can buy more; another can refuse more; the old winner question no longer has one scale.

## Current judgment

At this point, **022 is the strongest narrative spine and 025 is the strongest ethical counterweight**. They should be replayed together as one converging historical trunk rather than kept as two isolated novels.

This is the first point in the project where I would stop expanding the multiverse. More breadth is now lower value than deeper replay of this trunk.

The next expensive pass should replay the trunk continuously, not as isolated beats: preserve memory across decisions, let relationship damage accumulate, and force later choices to inherit earlier money/time/institution costs.
