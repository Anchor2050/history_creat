# 2026–2030 handoff validation v1

Purpose: test whether the repaired first five years actually earn the January 2030 state used by the frozen 2030–2035 trunk.

## What changed

The original eighteen-node spine remains intact. Sixteen connective nodes were added only where an otherwise important post-2030 variable lacked lived provenance:

- housing/lease decisions;
- family coordination before acute care;
- ordinary workload/health limits;
- benefit continuity at job or city transitions;
- geographic option maintenance;
- Xu's cash/housing reason for accepting verification work;
- low-drama relationship maintenance and drift.

The repair deliberately does not add marriage, childbirth, home purchase, severe illness, disaster, sudden wealth or a newly invented betrayal. Those remain possible branches, not prerequisites for a complete adulthood history.

## Five-year continuity after repair

### 2026 — scarcity leaves material residue
Chen's administrative exception and graduation still form the spine. The added history makes her first salary do three things at once: rebuild her own buffer, begin regular family support and leave housing mostly unchanged. Lin's response is also material: she pays more rent to avoid a long commute, buying time and low lock-in rather than a better home.

The year therefore ends with two different conversions already visible:
- Chen: income -> buffer + family capacity;
- Lin: cash -> time + mobility.

Neither conversion is yet known to be wise.

### 2027 — material inequality enters both friendship and family
The income-comparison wound remains. Its repair no longer occurs only in one apology scene: an awkward ordinary meal shows that the friendship continues while spontaneity falls.

Chen also begins doing small administrative tasks for her parents. This is intentionally mundane. Her parents are self-managing; she is becoming the person who can navigate digital/administrative friction. The historical residue is a coordination role, not a prewritten illness.

By year end Chen's retraining has created time poverty, Lin's slower path looks materially weak, and Xu remains in low-status reproduction work. Nothing spectacular has to happen.

### 2028 — options acquire maintenance costs
Lin's refusal of a high-paid invisible-verification role now has a follow-up: she probes an external-city research option and spends time learning whether its autonomy is real. Mobility becomes maintained infrastructure rather than personality.

Chen's apparently correct retraining strategy creates an ordinary bodily limit: she turns down extra weekend work because sleep and attention are deteriorating. Income growth and exercisable capacity separate.

Xu's low-cost lease and later verification-job choice add the material side his previous arc lacked. Stable pay and continuous medical insurance are part of the decision; he does not accept the role because the story has told him verification will become historically important.

### 2029 — the old adulthood bundle visibly separates
Lin renews only one year and reserves cash for deposit/move discontinuity. She later asks an outside employer about contract gaps and social-insurance continuity before asking whether a move is truly feasible. Housing stability and geographic option value are now in direct tension.

Chen's routine family coordination becomes more practiced while remaining bounded. At work she protects paid training by delaying lower-priority work, proving that humane rules consume budget before 2030.

Xu and Lin suffer the first costly provenance conflict. They keep working together, but the relationship becomes more professional and less easy. Xu also uses some new income to buy back sleep rather than upgrade status consumption.

The year closes quietly. Relationships persist at lower frequency. This matters because January 2030's rejection of the 'who is winning?' question now comes after years of unequal cash, time, housing and professional trajectories rather than appearing as an authorial thesis statement.

### January 2030 — state, not verdict
No common winner exists, but this is not because everyone has learned the correct philosophy.

Lin has moderate cash, low housing lock-in and a costly but real mobility option. Chen has the most income and internal authority, less unowned time, a growing family-coordination role and budget-limited power to be humane. Xu has stable specialist work and benefits, a low-status housing path, growing epistemic authority and less power to set agendas.

All three have gained something that does not cleanly convert into the thing another person has.

## Counterfactual attacks

### A. Remove Chen's pre-2030 family coordination (`p04`, `p11`)
2031 acute illness remains possible, but care would arrive as a new plot subsystem. The later mechanism still works, yet the biography becomes less continuous.

Decision: keep the coordination nodes. They explain transmission channels without predicting illness.

### B. Remove Lin's external probes (`p07`, `p15`)
Her 2030 rent decision still makes financial sense through `p10`, but later claims that she preserved mobility become mostly personality language. We would know she *could* move only because the author says so.

Decision: keep the probes. They turn optionality into maintained, partially priced infrastructure.

### C. Make Lin buy a home in 2029
The post-2030 story still works, but h31 must change completely and her early-2030 mobility becomes lower. This is a valid alternative world, not a contradiction of the trunk.

Decision: home purchase remains a live counterfactual branch; renting is not canonized as morally superior.

### D. Remove Xu's material reason for the verification job (`p06`, `p09`)
His epistemic arc survives, but he becomes too much of a thematic instrument: he chooses verification because verification matters to the novel.

Decision: keep the cash/benefit/housing constraints. They make a historically meaningful later role emerge from an ordinary constrained job decision.

### E. Remove Chen's workload limit (`p08`)
The work arc still functions. However the first five years again imply that time poverty is purely scheduling rather than bodily capacity.

Decision: retain one non-diagnostic capacity limit, while refusing to invent a major illness merely for depth.

### F. Repair Lin/Chen and Lin/Xu instantly after each conflict
Causal outcomes remain possible, but trust becomes event-based and too clean. Later relationship damage would feel newly manufactured.

Decision: keep awkward meals, lower contact frequency and professionalized trust. Repair does not restore the previous relationship state.

## Remaining incompleteness

The repair closes the structural handoff, but it does **not** mean every month of 2026–2030 deserves a scene. Three areas should stay deliberately underdetermined until scene replay needs them:

1. exact RMB income/rent/savings amounts;
2. exact parental ages/medical histories beyond what a scene requires;
3. romance/childbearing branches.

Inventing these now merely to make the database look complete would produce false precision and reduce future freedom.

## Judgment

The answer to the audit question was yes: **the five years before 2030 were present as a strong event spine but incomplete as continuous life history.**

After the connective replay, the most important structural gap is closed. The next useful pre-2030 work is literary replay of selected formative scenes and counterfactual branching, not another grand social thesis.
