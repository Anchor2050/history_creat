# Rival history replay 2035 v1

## Scope
This pass compares two continuous histories generated from the frozen 2030–2035 trunk:

- **Alpha — quiet rupture**: ordinary macro conditions, unrepaired Lin/Xu rupture, Xu's recognition arriving during reduced health capacity.
- **Beta — exercised mobility**: Lin actually moves, gains bargaining power, then discovers that cross-city care and welfare continuity are part of the price.

The ordinal ledger values below are engineering parameters used to expose direction and conversion conflict. They are not forecasts, empirical magnitudes, utilities, or quantities that may be added across dimensions.

## Validation
Both histories satisfy the structural checks encoded in `rival_histories_2035_v1.py`:

- chronological event order;
- all non-trunk causal references resolve;
- every event carries a complete state vector;
- all deltas are explicitly labeled as engineering parameters;
- every event records what the actor knew locally at the time.

The local-information field matters. Later consequences are not allowed to leak backward and make an earlier decision artificially wise or foolish.

## Cross-history findings

### 1. Alpha is currently the stronger historical backbone
Not because it is darker. It is stronger because major changes occur without a cinematic macro shock and because all three character ledgers contain simultaneous gain and loss.

The most important Alpha sequence is:

`Lin's unused option -> shame and partial repair -> Xu refuses unverifiable backing -> smaller result survives -> relationship does not -> Xu's archive diffuses while his capacity falls -> the three do not receive a reunion scene.`

The ending works because nothing in it resolves the ledger.

### 2. Beta is a necessary live competitor, not a discarded branch
Beta protects the project from making hesitation the only serious form of adulthood. Lin acts. The move genuinely improves some dimensions. The story still acquires cost because cities are bundles and family coordination is spatial.

If Beta later becomes less aesthetically powerful at scene level, that is acceptable. It must lose because the lived history is weaker, not because the model prefers regret.

### 3. The strongest dramatic object is now trust, not income
Income still matters, but the richest multi-year conversions currently run through trust:

- Chen spends organizational trust to protect paid training and later rebuilds some of it by exposing failure;
- Xu converts evidentiary reliability into institutional trust while losing personal trust;
- Lin gains a more defensible claim while losing the ability to narrate the conflict as a clean moral lesson.

Trust is therefore promoted from texture to a first-class exchange resource in scene construction.

### 4. Absence is becoming more powerful than reunion
The current Alpha ending does not put the three characters in one room to explain the theme. Chen uses Xu's material; Lin leaves before the end; Xu never comes. Their history intersects institutionally while failing to converge socially.

This is a stronger beauty mechanism than a retrospective conversation because the same network is present through traces, documents, habits and changed standards.

## Adversarial attack before canonization
Alpha is not yet canon. It must survive at least three attacks:

1. **No mental-health episode for Xu** — if Alpha only works because recognition coincides with illness, the line is overengineered.
2. **Partial Lin/Xu repair in 2034** — if one believable act of repair destroys the long echo, the relationship rupture is too brittle.
3. **Chen's care load improves materially after 2032** — if her character loses meaning once care pressure eases, the arc was exploiting burden rather than tracking institutional life.

Beta must survive two attacks:

1. a destination city with clearly better care infrastructure;
2. a destination city with weaker labor bargaining power but cheaper housing.

The move should remain a bundle exchange under both, though the signs of individual components may change.

## Current selection decision
**Advance Alpha to scene-level literary replay as the provisional backbone. Keep Beta active as the principal counter-history. Do not canonize Alpha yet.**

The next pass should write scenes only at points where the ledger changes or where an old event changes meaning: 2032 shame/repair, 2033 refusal, 2034 training/failure echo, 2035 diffusion-with-absence. Ordinary connective life must remain between them so the history does not read like a sequence of designed plot points.
