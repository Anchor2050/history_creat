# Alpha / Beta adversarial pass 2035 v1

## Purpose
Attack the provisional Alpha backbone before any scene is canonized. The question is whether the line survives when convenient dramatic supports are removed.

## Alpha attack A1 — Xu has no mental-health episode

### Intervention
Remove the 2035 insomnia/anxiety reduction in public work. Xu remains healthy enough to attend meetings and pursue normal career options when the failure archive begins to diffuse.

### What changes
Recognition becomes more convertible. Xu can plausibly gain some bargaining power, invitations and professional legitimacy from the archive.

### Does Alpha collapse?
No.

The relationship loss and institutional diffusion still survive. The important historical claim was never that recognition must arrive during illness. It was that usefulness, recognition, pay, trust and flourishing do not move in lockstep.

A healthy Xu can receive modest professional benefit while still carrying the cost of the Lin rupture and additional verification responsibility. The archive may become useful to people who do not know or reward its originator fully.

### Revision forced
**Remove illness as a load-bearing beauty mechanism.**

Health can remain part of the state vector, but Alpha's final absence must not require Xu to be unwell. If he does not attend the 2035 meeting, there must be an ordinary locally rational reason: another obligation, a scheduling conflict, lack of interest, or a relationship boundary.

This makes the ending stronger.

---

## Alpha attack A2 — Lin and Xu partially repair in 2034

### Intervention
After nearly a year of no personal contact, Lin sends Xu a narrow technical question. He answers. They can work together on bounded matters again but do not recover the previous friendship.

### What changes
The claim of permanent rupture is too strong. Real relationships often reclassify rather than switch from friendship to zero contact.

### Does Alpha collapse?
No. It improves.

The durable cost becomes **loss of intimacy and default trust**, not theatrical estrangement. They can share standards without regaining the old permission structure of friendship.

The 2033 refusal therefore changes the topology of the relationship:

`friendship with professional overlap -> rupture -> bounded professional contact without restored intimacy`.

This is more historically credible and creates a subtler echo in 2035. Lin can be present in a room where Xu's work is used and still have no natural reason to seek him out afterward.

### Revision forced
Replace `friendship_rupture` as a permanent state with **relationship reclassification**. The cost survives; melodrama decreases.

## Alpha attack A3 — Chen's care load materially improves after 2032

### Intervention
Formal care becomes more reliable, her father's condition stabilizes, and another family member takes a larger coordination role. Chen regains some weekends and can occasionally take uninterrupted leave.

### What changes
Her usable-time ledger recovers. She is no longer defined by continuous depletion. A plausible promotion or project expansion becomes available again.

### Does Alpha collapse?
No.

Her strongest historical function moves away from suffering and toward **institutional conversion**: can authority, trust and recovered time be turned into a durable paid-training practice, or do budgets and organizational priorities still limit it?

The earlier care period remains consequential because it changes what she values and what she is willing to promise. But the story no longer needs to keep punishing her in order to remain meaningful.

### Revision forced
By 2033–2034 Chen should regain some time and health capacity. Her later constraint should come partly from organizational tradeoffs, not only family burden.

This is a goodness improvement: the world is allowed to help people without destroying the story.

# Beta attack B1 — destination city has clearly better care infrastructure

### Intervention
Lin's destination provides better hospital access, more reliable formal care coordination and easier remote administrative handling than expected.

### Result
The care penalty of migration falls sharply. The move becomes more favorable.

Beta survives because the bundle still changes: proximity to specific family members, accumulated local trust, welfare/administrative continuity and old professional ties are not identical to formal service capacity.

**Important:** the model must permit the move to be genuinely good overall in many plausible futures. Bundle exchange does not mean every exchange must be tragic.

---

# Beta attack B2 — destination has cheap housing but weaker labor bargaining

### Intervention
Rent/housing security improves substantially after the move, while the new institution offers less labor-market outside power than expected.

### Result
The signs rotate: housing improves, bargaining gain shrinks or reverses, mobility initially rises and later falls as Lin settles.

Beta again survives. There is no one-dimensional city ranking.

---

# What the attacks remove

The following attractive claims are rejected:

1. Xu must be ill when recognition arrives.
2. Lin and Xu must remain completely estranged forever.
3. Chen must remain care-exhausted through 2035.
4. Lin's move must carry a major care penalty in every destination.

These were branch conveniences, not trunk necessities.

# Alpha v2 shape

The provisional stronger Alpha now has this form:

- 2030: Lin preserves mobility; Chen starts paid training.
- 2031: Chen's family care and budget pressure expose conversion limits; Xu gains a formal verification role.
- 2032: Lin allows one outside option to decay and experiences status shame; she partially repairs with Chen.
- 2033: Xu refuses unverifiable backing; Lin shrinks the project; their friendship breaks.
- 2034: Chen's care burden eases somewhat and she uses recovered capacity to rebuild a smaller, more honest training practice. Lin and Xu regain bounded professional contact without restored intimacy.
- 2035: Xu's archive/checklist diffuses and gives him some professional benefit, but not a moral settlement. The three histories intersect through standards and training; no forced reunion occurs.

This version is less dramatic event-by-event and more robust under counterfactual change.

# Decision
**Alpha survives the adversarial pass, but only after losing three convenient dramatic supports. Advance Alpha v2, not Alpha v1, to scene-level replay.**

Beta also survives and remains the principal counter-history. It must stay alive until scene-level comparison because it proves that a more agentic Lin can inhabit the same historical trunk.
