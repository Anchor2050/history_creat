# History convergence — final pass for this session

## Iteration chain completed

This session did not stop after the first forecast expansion.

1. Broadened research beyond technology into demand/property, welfare, aging/care, fertility/family, health/mental health, education/credentials, urban form, regional mobility, climate resilience and trade fragmentation.
2. Rebuilt the forecast world around five correlated futures.
3. Stress-tested eight previously important story claims.
4. Rejected the earlier technology-centered thesis as too narrow for the historical spine.
5. Froze four more robust social claims.
6. Attacked those claims with twelve non-technology shocks.
7. Reclassified character victories as conditional while preserving the social mechanism.

## What has converged

For the 2026-2030 generation window, the world model now converges on a historical object:

> **The old adulthood bundle is decomposing. Education, stable work, income, housing, family formation, city belonging and long-run security cease to be one synchronized ladder. What matters increasingly is not only what a person owns or earns, but how many meaningful options remain feasible after care, housing, benefits, health, geography, institutional rules and labor-market shocks are accounted for.**

This is deliberately broader than an AI thesis. AI can accelerate it, but aging, property adjustment, welfare reform, urban restructuring, credential repricing, regional divergence and external volatility can all produce the same deeper separation.

## What has not converged

We should not freeze any of these as destiny:

- Chen definitely becomes a high-income winner;
- AI definitely destroys her first post-PhD role;
- Xu definitely earns a premium for verification;
- stronger public care definitely arrives in time for a particular family;
- Lin definitely preserves research autonomy;
- paid junior training survives a weak-demand firm budget;
- marriage, birth or home ownership completes anyone's adulthood.

These remain world-contingent.

## Consequence for simulation

The simulator should now generate **option sets**, not predetermined arcs.

Each year, the world first updates:
- household security and care liability;
- housing/rent/public-service access;
- labor demand and benefit quality;
- institutional headcount/evaluation/training capacity;
- regional job density and migration cost;
- health/time capacity;
- climate disruption;
- external client/funding volatility.

Only then may a character choose from locally known feasible options.

A later historian/writer may select a history because it is beautiful. The world generator may not make it happen because it would be beautiful.

## New meaning of 真善美

**真** now means more than internal causal consistency: a life path must remain plausible under a research-grounded social world.

**善** becomes institutional as well as personal. A person may want to act well and lack budget, time or authority. The morally important event can be the failed attempt to preserve humane rules.

**美** comes from delayed reinterpretation. A house once read as success may later become immobility; renting may become freedom or precarity; a degree may become knowledge without security; a refusal may become either autonomy or a luxury the person later loses.

## Stop condition reached for world-thesis discovery

Further research is still valuable, but the marginal question has changed. New sources are now more likely to change scenario weights, city/family texture and branch outcomes than to replace the four frozen social mechanisms.

That is sufficient convergence for the **world thesis**. It is not convergence of the characters' histories.

The next compute phase should therefore spend less on discovering another grand trend and more on growing many detailed lives inside this converged world, then extracting the histories that survive truth/goodness/beauty review.
