# Convergence record — 2026-10-11

## Session result

The iteration has converged far enough to freeze the **world thesis**, while leaving character histories open.

The research loop moved through:

technology repricing -> broad social forecast -> correlated futures -> cross-future stress -> adversarial social shocks -> 20,000 engineering stress worlds -> resource-conversion history model.

The important discovery is that the historical unit is not the event and not the promotion. It is the **conversion**.

Can education become security? Can money become time? Can housing become stability without destroying mobility? Can expertise become bargaining power? Can welfare become practical freedom? Can moral intention become an institution? When conversion fails, who absorbs the cost?

## Frozen world thesis

**2026-2030 is modeled as a period in which the old adulthood bundle increasingly decomposes.** Education, occupation, income, housing, family formation, city belonging and long-run security can move in different directions. The key inequality is option value: who can convert one resource into another without unacceptable cost.

This thesis survived five correlated futures and twelve non-technology adversarial shocks in the engineering model.

## Character histories remain open

Chen may become richer without becoming freer. Lin may preserve autonomy only by accepting material cost. Xu may become socially useful without becoming well rewarded. Stronger welfare can change any of these paths. A care shock can reverse them. A city move can solve one constraint while creating another.

None of those outcomes is frozen.

## Compute allocation after convergence

Stop spending most compute on discovering additional grand theses. Allocate future compute approximately as:

- 55% detailed endogenous life growth inside the converged social world;
- 20% household/family/relationship autonomy and memory;
- 10% city/material-life realization;
- 10% institutional/organizational evolution;
- 5% continuing forecast refresh and falsification.

This is an engineering allocation, not a runtime guarantee.

## History-mining target

Mine chains of conversions, especially:

- successful conversion followed by an unexpected loss elsewhere;
- blocked conversion that later becomes possible;
- private gain produced by transferred social/family cost;
- good intention blocked by lack of authority/budget;
- public policy that changes a private feasible set without determining the choice;
- ordinary material change whose meaning is recognized years later.

The project is now ready to grow lives rather than keep inventing theses about them.
