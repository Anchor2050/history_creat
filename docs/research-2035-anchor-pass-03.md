# Research 2035 anchor pass 03 — retirement, health, climate/energy

Status: final trunk-changing evidence attack requested by convergence pass 02.

This pass asks whether retirement/social-security reform, physical/mental health, and climate/energy transition require a new 2026–2035 causal skeleton. It distinguishes observed policy/targets from project scenarios.

## 1. Retirement and social security

### Observed policy anchor
China's gradual statutory retirement-age reform took effect on 1 January 2025. The statutory retirement age is raised gradually for men and women according to the published schedule. From 1 January 2030, the minimum contribution period for receiving the basic pension rises from 15 years toward 20 years, increasing by six months per year. Flexible early or delayed retirement is permitted within specified bounds.

Source: National People's Congress / official government publication on implementation of gradual retirement-age reform.

### What this changes in the world
The important historical variable is not simply “people work longer.” The reform changes several exchange rates at once:

1. **older-worker time -> household care availability**: later retirement can reduce the amount of routine grandparent care available to adult children before formal retirement, though actual effects vary by household and job;
2. **continuous covered employment -> pension security**: contribution continuity becomes more valuable when the minimum qualifying period rises;
3. **late-career earnings -> family transfers**: a working parent may preserve household cash while offering fewer daytime care hours;
4. **retirement flexibility -> bargaining**: some families gain options, but those options depend on health, employer agreement, savings and contribution history.

These mechanisms cut both ways. Delayed retirement can strengthen household balance sheets and reduce dependence on adult children while simultaneously reducing available elder/child-care time.

### Trunk attack
This does not overturn the existing care/family mechanism. It strengthens the distinction between **cash support** and **time support**. A household can gain pension security while losing informal care hours. The old adulthood bundle becomes more decomposed, not less.

For Chen, the relevant branch is no longer “parent retired = available.” Parent work status, health and contribution incentives all enter the care network. Her contingent-time liability can therefore arise even in a household with improving retirement income.

For Lin, employment continuity and benefit history become part of migration pricing. A job or city move remains a bundle decision rather than a salary comparison.

## 2. Physical and mental health

### Observed policy anchors
China's National Health Commission designated 2025–2027 as a three-year “Pediatrics and Mental Health Service Year” campaign. The stated 2027 goal includes broader psychological, sleep and mental-health outpatient availability and improved public awareness and help-seeking.

The Healthy China 2030 framework continues to treat chronic disease, healthy ageing, mental health and lowering personal health expenditure burden as long-run policy goals. The framework explicitly treats chronic disease and ageing as major population-health burdens.

Sources: National Health Commission 2025–2027 Pediatrics and Mental Health Service Year action plan; Healthy China 2030 planning documents.

### What this changes in the world
Health should enter the simulation as **capacity and variance**, not only diagnosis.

A useful state decomposition is:

`health state -> predictable capacity + high-variance bad days -> appointment/treatment time -> out-of-pocket cost -> work flexibility -> care received/provided -> ability to exercise options`

For mental health:

`stress / sleep / anxiety / depression symptoms -> concentration + avoidance + relationship friction + help-seeking -> treatment access / stigma / time cost -> work and mobility choices`

The project should not assign a mental-health condition merely to make a character deep. The historically important point is that service access may improve while work design, housing pressure, care load and career uncertainty continue producing stress. Better treatment access changes the severity and duration of some branches; it does not erase upstream causes.

### Trunk attack
A health shock can temporarily collapse a person's option set more sharply than money alone predicts. This initially looks like a possible new skeleton. On inspection it fits the existing concept of **exercisable-option capacity**: cash, benefits, time, health, housing, family and networks jointly determine whether an option can actually be used.

Health therefore changes weights and timing rather than requiring a new organizing principle.

It does require one correction: the project must not treat “having an option” as independent of fluctuating physical/mental capacity. Option value is state-dependent and can decay intermittently, not only structurally.

## 3. Climate and energy transition

### Observed policy/target anchors
China submitted its 2035 nationally determined contribution in November 2025. The announced 2035 targets include:

- economy-wide net greenhouse-gas emissions 7%–10% below peak levels, striving to do better;
- non-fossil energy above 30% of total energy consumption;
- wind and solar installed capacity above six times the 2020 level, striving for 3,600 GW;
- new-energy vehicles becoming the mainstream of new vehicle sales;
- national carbon-market coverage of major high-emitting sectors;
- basic establishment of a climate-adaptive society.

Sources: UNFCCC China 2035 NDC registry; Ministry of Foreign Affairs / Ministry of Ecology and Environment official announcements, September–November 2025.

These are policy targets, not guaranteed realized outcomes.

### What this changes in the world
Climate/energy should enter ordinary biography through several channels:

1. **heat / extreme weather -> commute, sleep and work capacity**;
2. **flooding / adaptation -> neighborhood reliability, insurance/repair burden, municipal spending and housing choice**;
3. **energy transition -> sectoral job creation/destruction and regional opportunity shifts**;
4. **building retrofit / cooling / electricity reliability -> household operating cost and comfort**;
5. **carbon policy / industrial restructuring -> employer budgets and geographic labor demand**.

The strongest historical effect is therefore not a “climate plot.” It is another repricing layer on the city bundle and on occupational geography.

### Trunk attack
A strong regional green-industry boom could make one secondary city materially more attractive to Lin. That changes the probability she moves, but the decision is still priced through job quality + network + rent/housing + partner/family + health + welfare + future options.

Repeated heat or flood disruption could make a cheap neighborhood expensive in time and reliability, changing housing choice. Again this modifies the bundle price rather than restoring a single success ladder.

A carbon-intensive employer facing restructuring may cut headcount or training budgets. That makes Chen's ability to institutionalize humane training more fragile, reinforcing the existing “goodness requires slack” mechanism.

## 4. Combined counterfactual stress cases

### R1 — later retirement, weaker grandparent-care availability
Parent income stays stronger while routine household care time falls. Chen's family has more cash but fewer available hours. Main skeleton survives; cash and time diverge more clearly.

### R2 — flexible retirement plus good health
A parent voluntarily retires earlier within allowed rules because the household can afford it. Chen gains care coverage and can accept an outside role. This weakens one branch, not the trunk: care liability remains contingent on household-specific conversion capacity.

### H1 — non-catastrophic recurring health limitation
A character has repeated poor-sleep, migraine, musculoskeletal or anxiety-related low-capacity periods without a dramatic diagnosis. Some nominal options become unusable for weeks. This strengthens the need to track capacity but does not change the central exchange-rate structure.

### H2 — better mental-health access
Earlier treatment reduces duration/severity of a difficult period. The biography becomes less tragic. The trunk still works because the relevant mechanism is whether institutional support converts distress into restored capacity before work/relationship losses become sticky.

### C1 — severe summer heat and commute disruption
A low-rent long commute loses attractiveness; a smaller expensive home near transit may produce more usable time. Housing remains an exchange among money, time, health and mobility.

### C2 — regional green-industry boom
A non-Beijing city gains attractive professional demand. Lin's move probability rises, Chen may remain locally anchored, Xu's methods may travel without his life moving. Portable expertise and portable biography remain different.

### C3 — transition-budget squeeze
An employer in a restructuring sector preserves core staff but cuts junior-training budget. Chen's ethical rule fails despite personal commitment. Goodness remains institutionally constrained.

## 5. Does any of this overturn the 2026–2035 causal skeleton?

No.

The new evidence changes important local probabilities and adds missing state variables, but all three domains map into mechanisms already present after pass 02:

- **retirement/social security** changes the conversion between working years, pension security, family cash and informal care time;
- **health** changes whether nominal options are actually exercisable at a given time;
- **climate/energy** changes housing reliability, occupational geography, city bundles, organizational budgets and health/time costs.

None restores the old synchronized adulthood ladder. None requires technology to be the central historical driver. None makes a single winner metric coherent again.

## 6. Required trunk corrections before final freeze

1. Define exercisable-option capacity as explicitly **health-state dependent**, including short-lived capacity collapses.
2. Treat retirement status as a family-time and income variable, not a binary marker of “available grandparent care.”
3. Add climate reliability to city/housing bundle pricing: commute, cooling, disruption and building/neighborhood resilience.
4. Keep 2035 climate outcomes as targets/scenarios rather than guaranteed realized history.
5. Continue prohibiting diagnosis, disaster or retirement decisions inserted only to manufacture theme.

## Judgment

This final evidence attack changes texture, branch probability and state accounting, but does not change the main social mechanisms, character-level causal skeleton or core historical question.

On the research criterion set in convergence pass 02, the 2026–2035 world is now eligible for final convergence review.
