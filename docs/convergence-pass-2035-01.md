# Convergence pass 2035-01

## Result
Not converged yet. The trunk became deeper and narrower.

## What survived the first 2035 research attack
1. The old adulthood success bundle keeps decomposing into partially independent resources: income, time, housing security, mobility, care capacity, status, bargaining power and welfare-backed refusal power.
2. Autonomy is not a personality trait. It is produced by buffers, welfare, housing, health, family obligations and labor-market outside options.
3. Social usefulness and market reward can diverge for long periods. Verification is the current test case, not a guaranteed winner.
4. Care is now a first-class historical mechanism. It can reverse the meaning of earlier income success by consuming time and migration options.
5. Good intentions need institutional slack. Chen's paid-training ethic is meaningful precisely because it can hit a budget ceiling and fail.
6. The stronger historical frame is now institutional asynchrony: different systems reprice adulthood at different speeds, creating conversion lags and stranded adaptations.

## What was demoted
- 'second graduation' remains a powerful Chen motif but is not the whole era.
- 'responsibility becomes expensive' is conditional; responsibility can become necessary without becoming well-paid.
- 'Lin chose autonomy' is too flattering. Keeping options open is not the same as exercising them; h36 intentionally makes her lose an opportunity through fear.
- 'Xu's failure archive pays off' is conditional. The stable claim is that it can create trust and institutional usefulness before pay catches up.

## New 2030-2035 historical arc
2030: options are still visible. Lin preserves mobility; Chen tries to convert authority into paid training.
2031: care and weak budgets attack both forms of freedom. Chen's income cannot purchase all lost time; her good institution stops expanding.
2032: Lin proves that autonomy can decay through non-use. Old status comparison returns through shame rather than direct income competition.
2033: Xu refuses to convert friendship into evidentiary waiver. Lin accepts a smaller, verifiable claim.
2034: the failure archive crosses institutional boundaries; Chen loses a promotion to care constraints and finally envies Lin's mobility.
2035: formal recognition arrives after the social need. No one gets a final settlement; the surviving historical object is the ledger of conversions and conversion lags.

## Truth / goodness / beauty review
Truth: improved because macro trends now transmit through budgets, care hours, hiring, housing and welfare rather than exposition. Still noncanonical until World authority replay.
Goodness: improved because Chen's paid-training project can fail, Xu's refusal hurts a friend, and Lin must pay for a smaller claim. No virtue-reward guarantee.
Beauty: strongest new echo is 2026 administrative scarcity -> 2029 refusal to externalize trial costs -> 2031 budget ceiling -> 2034 failure archive used for training. A second echo is 2027 income wound -> 2032 reversed status shame -> 2034 Chen envies mobility. These need counterfactual validation before canonization.

## Why not converged
Three uncertainties can still change the trunk, not just texture:
1. City-level divergence: Beijing versus plausible destination cities may overturn Lin's mobility story.
2. Care institutions: long-term-care insurance/service expansion may materially change Chen's care-time constraint.
3. Gender/family formation: unpaid-care allocation and partnership/childbearing choices are under-modeled and could dominate 2030-2035 life paths.

Next pass should spend research budget on those three, then run the listed counterfactuals. Do not broaden to more generic macro themes unless evidence changes one of these mechanisms.
