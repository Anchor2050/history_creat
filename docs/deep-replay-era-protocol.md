# Deep replay: era protocol

Deep replay must feel like lives inside history, not characters inside a sealed laboratory.

## Two clocks

Every replay has two clocks:

1. **known history** — dated facts that have happened by the replay date;
2. **future possibility** — plausible waves with conditions and uncertainty.

The second clock can change expectations and decisions, but cannot become a fact until the world branch realizes it.

## 2026 observed anchors

The initial evidence set includes China's AI+ program; 2026 graduate-employment and national recruitment policies; evidence on uneven GenAI productivity and risks to younger-worker opportunity; the shift from chatbot work toward multi-step agents; recurring cross-occupation AI task use; frontier AI work in mathematics/formal proof and professional computer workflows; and October 2026 first-home financing support.

These anchors should reach characters through consequences: a changed job description, a lab buying agent access, a supervisor changing what counts as independent work, a company shrinking junior analyst hiring while opening AI-operations roles, a student learning Lean because verification becomes valuable, a family reconsidering buying versus renting. Naming a company or policy without a consequence is not worldbuilding.

## 2027–2030 scenario waves

The engine carries conditional branches for:

- agents becoming standard knowledge-work coworkers;
- accelerated AI-for-science and formal verification;
- recomposition of graduate/junior roles;
- Chinese advanced-manufacturing and digital-economy expansion;
- possible housing balance-sheet repair;
- compute/energy constraints and unequal AI access;
- a possible productivity dividend if gains diffuse beyond frontier firms.

Good and bad consequences must coexist. The same wave may increase a small team's power and destroy a training ladder; accelerate science and devalue a hard-earned routine skill; create new industrial jobs while making academic careers less attractive; reduce some housing financing costs while leaving mobility and family obligations unequal.

## Large companies

Real companies may appear when historically known and materially relevant: model releases, cloud/compute access, hiring practices, software ecosystems, research tooling, platform rules, prices or procurement. The simulation must not invent future company announcements. Future corporate behavior is a scenario branch with provenance.

## Literary rule

The era should usually be visible indirectly. A character notices that a job posting changed, an old skill stopped commanding a premium, a paper can be checked differently, a team of three now does work that required ten, a parent changes advice, rent versus ownership arithmetic changes, or a colleague moves into a newly expanding industry. This is stronger than paragraphs explaining 'the AI revolution'.

The target is **private life under historical pressure**: large forces change feasible sets, then people still have to choose.
