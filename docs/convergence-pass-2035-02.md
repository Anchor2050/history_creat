# Convergence pass 2035-02 — final structural attack

## Question
Can retirement/social insurance, physical and mental health, or climate/energy shocks overturn the 2030–2035 trunk established in pass 01?

## Evidence constraints added

### Retirement and social insurance
China's gradual retirement-age reform began in 2025. Retirement is now partly an interval rather than a single fixed transition: eligible workers may retire early within limits, while delayed retirement can extend up to three years by agreement. From 2030, the minimum contribution period for monthly basic pension begins rising from 15 toward 20 years. This means late-career freedom depends on contribution history, employment status, bargaining power, health, and the ability to bridge income before pension receipt.

Historical consequence: pension eligibility is another conversion institution. The same accumulated work history can convert into very different amounts of usable time depending on health, care obligations and employer bargaining power. It strengthens the existing conversion-lag frame rather than replacing it.

### Physical and mental health
Health is promoted from background texture to a state variable. A person's option set is constrained by stamina, sleep, chronic illness, disability, mental distress, access to care and the unpredictability of episodes. Policy expansion of psychological and mental-health services during 2025–2027 makes access plausibly better, but service availability does not imply recovery or equal access.

Historical consequence: money, status and mobility are not fully fungible with bodily capacity. A high-income character can possess nominal options that cannot actually be exercised. This is a stronger formulation of the distinction between option ownership and option exercise already exposed by Lin's 2032 branch.

### Climate and energy shocks
Do not model climate as a cinematic apocalypse by default. Use it as a distribution of interruptions: heat, flooding, transport failure, electricity constraints, insurance/property loss, caregiving emergencies and uneven workplace exposure. These shocks hit people through housing quality, occupation, commuting, health, savings and local infrastructure.

Historical consequence: climate risk reprices buffers. Housing, savings, flexible work, family proximity and local public capacity acquire contingent value. It therefore tests the conversion ledger under correlated shocks rather than supplying a separate master narrative.

## Counterfactual attacks

### R1 — retirement flexibility becomes materially generous
Suppose pension and retirement flexibility improve enough that Chen's parents can purchase more formal care and one parent can stop work earlier. Chen recovers time. Her 2031 care squeeze weakens substantially.

Result: Chen's exact plot changes. The trunk survives because care capacity was never defined as care hours alone; it is a bundle of money, services, kin availability, health and institutional eligibility. Better policy changes the conversion rate.

### R2 — retirement reform produces a contribution-history trap
Suppose an older relative has fragmented employment and must remain economically active longer than expected to secure pension eligibility. Care now flows in both directions: Chen provides coordination while the older relative cannot become a full-time family caregiver.

Result: strengthens conversion lag and unequal reversibility. No new master mechanism required.

### H1 — Lin develops a persistent but non-catastrophic health limitation
She retains credentials, savings and nominal mobility, but long travel, relocation and high-intensity work become costly. The job she once kept as an outside option is no longer an equivalent option.

Result: this kills any remaining interpretation of autonomy as a stock of credentials or cash. Autonomy must be measured as *exercisable option capacity* at a particular time.

### H2 — Xu has a mental-health episode during institutional recognition
The failure archive begins to receive attention precisely when Xu has less capacity to capitalize on it. Colleagues can use the archive while Xu cannot fully convert recognition into status or income.

Result: the story becomes less meritocratic and more historically credible. Social usefulness, recognition, income and personal flourishing can diverge in both directions and at different times.

### C1 — severe heat/flood interruption in one city
A short but serious disruption makes remote-work flexibility, nearby kin, cooling, insurance and emergency cash suddenly valuable. Lin's supposedly inferior smaller-city option can outperform a higher-status metropolitan job for several months, or the reverse if the smaller city has weaker infrastructure.

Result: city ranking remains bundle-dependent. No stable one-dimensional 'better city' ordering survives.

### C2 — correlated climate + care shock
A parent becomes ill during a transport/heat disruption. Chen's problem is no longer simply money or care hours; coordination reliability collapses. A modest nearby network becomes more valuable than a larger distant income increment.

Result: reinforces the concept of high-variance time debt and exposes a missing term: *coordination robustness*.

### C3 — climate/energy conditions remain mostly manageable through 2035
No major personal disaster occurs. Heat and resilience spending change costs and routines but do not dominate the characters' lives.

Result: trunk still survives. Climate need not fire dramatically to be represented honestly.

## Structural revisions forced by this pass

1. Replace `reversible-option capacity` with **exercisable reversible-option capacity**: an option counts only to the extent that health, time, money, legal/institutional eligibility and coordination make it usable.
2. Add **coordination robustness** to care capacity. Average available hours are insufficient when failures are correlated or emergencies cluster.
3. Treat pension/social insurance rights as conversion infrastructure, alongside housing, welfare and labor institutions.
4. Keep climate as a shock process acting through existing institutions and buffers unless future evidence shows a genuine regime break.
5. Explicitly permit recognition to arrive when the person who created the useful object is unable to capitalize on it.

## Convergence decision

**Structural convergence reached for the 2030–2035 trunk.**

The final attack changes state variables, conversion rates, and event probabilities, but it does not produce a stronger replacement for the surviving causal frame:

> adulthood is increasingly governed by asynchronous institutions that convert income, time, health, housing, care, mobility, status and welfare rights at different rates; people experience history through the options they can actually exercise, the conversions they can or cannot make, and the lag between social usefulness and private reward.

This is now the frozen structural trunk for 2030–2035. Future research may alter it only if evidence breaks a causal mechanism. New facts that merely add texture should enter the world state, scenes, or branch probabilities without reopening the trunk.

## Canon rule after convergence
Do not canonize the most elegant branch. Canonize only events that survive character causality, institutional feasibility and counterfactual competition. The world engine should now generate multiple concrete histories from this fixed trunk and select for dramatic pressure without changing the historical mechanism to reward virtue or maximize plot neatness.
