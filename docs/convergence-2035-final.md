# 2026–2035 historical convergence — final

Status: **CONVERGED** for the current evidence base and current character/world scope.

This report closes the research/replay phase that began with the 2026–2030 technology-centered world and was repeatedly widened and attacked through housing, care, family, welfare, city sorting, health, retirement, climate/energy and non-technology counterfactuals.

Convergence here does not mean that every scene is canon, every future event is fixed, or every character branch is resolved. It means the main social mechanisms, long-run causal skeletons and historical question have become stable enough that new high-quality evidence is now more likely to change texture, branch probability and local timing than replace the trunk.

---

## 1. Convergence criteria

The project required all of the following before declaring convergence.

### A. New research mostly changes texture rather than trunk
PASS.

The final evidence attack on retirement/social security, physical/mental health and climate/energy produced real corrections:

- retirement status must be split into income, contribution incentives and available family-care time;
- exercisable-option capacity must be explicitly health-state dependent;
- climate reliability must enter housing/city bundle pricing;
- climate targets remain targets/scenarios rather than guaranteed realized outcomes.

These corrections do not replace the causal skeleton.

### B. Key people have continuous histories to 2035
PASS.

The original 2026–2030 event spine was strong but under-lived. The pre-2030 repair added housing, family coordination, benefit continuity, health/time limits, city-option maintenance and ordinary relationship residue without rewriting the original eighteen key nodes.

The 2030–2035 replay then inherits these states rather than introducing care, mobility or trust as new themes after 2030.

### C. The trunk survives multiple non-technology shocks
PASS.

It survives:

- strong care improvement;
- weak care;
- partnership/child formation;
- housing recovery;
- severe downturn;
- regional boom outside Beijing;
- null dramatic-technology shock;
- later retirement / weaker grandparent care;
- better health-service access;
- recurring non-catastrophic health limitation;
- heat/commute disruption;
- green-industry geographic repricing;
- transition-related budget pressure.

Specific outcomes move. The mechanisms remain.

### D. Beautiful claims that fail counterfactuals are deleted
PASS.

The project has repeatedly removed attractive but overly deterministic events rather than preserving them for literary neatness.

## 2. Evidence classes

The world now distinguishes four epistemic classes.

### Observed facts / enacted policy
Examples include:

- population ageing and low fertility;
- continued urbanization alongside property adjustment;
- gradual statutory retirement-age reform from 2025;
- the rise in the pension minimum contribution period beginning in 2030;
- mental-health-service expansion initiatives in 2025–2027;
- China's submitted 2035 climate NDC;
- observed diffusion of AI/agentic tools into knowledge-work organizations.

These may constrain the world directly.

### Official targets / institutional forecasts
Examples include:

- Healthy China 2030 targets;
- 2035 climate targets;
- medium/long-run policy ambitions for care, urban renewal, low-carbon transition and public service.

These influence expectations and institutional investment but may not be treated as guaranteed outcomes.

### Scenario assumptions
Examples include:

- strong-care vs weak-care implementation;
- regional opportunity boom;
- housing recovery;
- severe weak-demand branch;
- partner/child branch;
- different rates of green-industry concentration;
- strong vs weak employer adaptation to AI.

These remain alternative futures.

### Engineering priors
Scenario weights, replay selection weights and screening thresholds are project tools. They are not empirical probabilities.

## 3. Frozen historical object

The converged object is:

> **From 2026 to 2035, the old adulthood bundle continues to decompose. Education, employment, income, housing, family formation, city belonging, health, care capacity, welfare security, professional status and retirement security no longer move as one synchronized ladder. People accumulate different buffers, but institutions reprice those buffers at different speeds. The decisive question becomes which options remain genuinely exercisable after money, time, health, care, housing, relationships, benefits, geography, power and trust are all charged against one another.**

Technology is one accelerator and one arena. It is not the sole historical cause.

The deeper era mechanism is **institutional asynchrony plus changing conversion rates among life resources**.

---

## 4. Frozen causal skeleton

1. Scarcity first appears in ordinary administrative forms: training slots, submission windows, jobs, housing security, attention, care time and organizational slack.
2. People respond by accumulating different buffers: credentials, cash, networks, reliability, housing claims, benefits, reciprocal relations and professional reputation.
3. Buffers are only partially convertible because institutions, markets and families reprice them at different speeds.
4. Health and care create state-dependent and high-variance time liabilities. Income can reduce some burdens without buying certainty.
5. Family bargains can begin as mutually reasonable arrangements and become structurally unequal when reversibility differs.
6. Migration is a bundle transaction across job quality, opportunity density, housing, commute, health, welfare, partner/family constraints and future options.
7. Organizations can discover socially useful work before pay/status systems price it correctly.
8. Personal goodness needs institutional slack. Humane choices may consume budget, authority or opportunity and can fail.
9. Climate/energy, retirement and health reforms alter exchange rates rather than restore a unified ladder.
10. By 2035 there is no coherent single scoreboard of adulthood success. Earlier choices are repeatedly reinterpreted as the exchange rates among resources change.

## 5. Character causal skeletons

### Chen Lan

2026: completes the doctorate under real administrative/family/cash pressure and uses an exception that indirectly costs Lin an option.

2026–27: converts education into material security faster than the others. Income improves, and the first class-like difference enters friendship.

2027–29: retrains toward scope, verification and responsibility; income rises while unowned time falls. She becomes the family member who handles complex administrative coordination before any major acute care event exists. She later gains enough organizational power to decide whether transition/training costs are pushed downward.

2030–35: her central problem is conversion, not simple upward mobility. Can income and authority be converted into care security, humane training and geographic freedom? Sometimes yes, sometimes no. A 2034 promotion remains conditional on the robustness of the care/household network.

Stable arc: **material ascent creates new capacities and new claims; power reveals who is made to pay transition costs.**

### Lin Jing

2026: mistakes autonomy for isolation and pays a real research-time cost.

2026–29: remains materially slower, but repeatedly spends cash/time to preserve lower housing lock-in and geographic/professional options. She probes outside opportunities and learns that a job offer is not automatically an exercisable option.

2030–35: her central problem is the maintenance and decay of option value. She may stay from fear, partner constraint, family proximity or rational city-bundle pricing; she may also move. No single cause is canonical.

Stable arc: **autonomy is not a personality virtue; it is maintained infrastructure whose exercise can become progressively more expensive.**

### Xu Hang

2026–28: occupies low-status reproduction/failure work while reliable execution becomes easier and more abundant. Material constraints matter: stable pay, benefits and housing cost shape his job choices.

2029–33: verification creates costly disagreement with Lin. Epistemic trust can increase while relational ease decreases.

2034–35: failure records may become training infrastructure, or may remain obscure internal work. Formal recognition is not required.

Stable arc: **social usefulness can precede compensation and status; trustworthy refusal can accumulate value without becoming a guaranteed career reward.**

## 6. The repaired 2026–2030 handoff

The five years before 2030 are no longer treated as a preface dominated by AI/career events.

Their durable state contributions are:

- Chen: cash buffer, family support role, time poverty, bodily capacity limits, client responsibility, budget-limited humane authority;
- Lin: transit-sensitive renting, low housing lock-in, maintained external-city contacts, benefit-friction knowledge, reserved mobility cash, relationship wounds that are repaired but not erased;
- Xu: low-cost housing, stable-benefit motive, failure archive, increasingly professionalized trust with Lin.

This creates a valid January 2030 state without inventing later illness, marriage, childbirth, home purchase or disaster in advance.

The absence of those events is deliberate underdetermination, not missing data.

## 9. Truth / goodness / beauty final gates

### 真 — PASS

The main causal skeleton survives multiple plausible social futures and does not depend on omniscient character choices. Major later states now have lived provenance in 2026–2030. Policy targets are separated from observed facts and project scenarios.

Remaining uncertainty is primarily about branch probability, timing, city-specific texture and individual biography.

### 善 — PASS, deliberately without moral guarantees

The model tracks who carries residual cost.

A good act can fail because there is no budget. A care service can help one household by shifting irregular labor to a low-paid worker. A mutually agreed family bargain can become unfair because one career is harder to restart. Independence is not treated as inherently superior to dependence or reciprocity.

The moral test is therefore not “did the good person win?” It is whether the history reveals hidden cost transfer and preserves the possibility of failed goodness.

### 美 — PASS at structural level

The decade now contains long echoes rather than isolated twists:

- the scarce 2026 administrative window returns later as Chen's power to allocate opportunity;
- the 2027 income wound changes how Lin and Chen discuss success years later;
- the 2028 external-job questions become the infrastructure of later mobility choices;
- the 2029 provenance dispute returns as a deeper 2033 trust conflict;
- material success can become geographic immobility;
- “temporary” family specialization can become biography;
- reliability work that looks pedantic can become institutional memory without needing a triumph scene.

The strongest recurring motif is **changing exchange rates**: the same resource means something different at different ages and under different institutions.

## 10. Core historical question

The earlier question — “when execution becomes cheap, what remains worth spending years of a life to obtain?” — remains useful but is now a sub-question.

The converged question is broader:

> **When the institutions that define adulthood stop moving in sync, what can a person actually convert one part of life into — and who pays when the conversion fails?**

This question survives without a dramatic AI revolution, without a housing crash, and without a single catastrophic family event.

---

## 11. Freeze rule after convergence

From this commit onward, the 2026–2035 **historical trunk is frozen** unless new evidence materially contradicts one of the frozen mechanisms.

Future scheduled runs should not manufacture new grand theses or repeatedly reconverge the same history.

They should do one of two things:

1. check genuinely new evidence for a trunk-breaking contradiction; or
2. deepen literary replay, character interiority, scene sequence and conditional branches without changing the world thesis unless the replay exposes a real causal contradiction.

A new source is trunk-breaking only if it plausibly shows that one or more of the following no longer holds across reasonable futures:

- adulthood resources remain only partially convertible;
- exercisable options depend on material/institutional/health/family conditions;
- care/family can create contingent-time liabilities and unequal reversibility;
- migration remains a multi-institution bundle decision;
- socially useful work can be mispriced for long periods;
- institutional slack determines whether personal ethics can become durable rules.

Absent such evidence, additional research should be recorded as texture or branch reweighting rather than used to reopen the entire architecture.

## Final decision

**The 2026–2035 historical world has converged.**

The next high-value phase is no longer world-thesis discovery. It is sustained literary replay inside this world: letting characters live, misread, repair, fail, move, stay, care, work and reinterpret earlier choices while preserving the causal ledger that has now survived the research attacks.
