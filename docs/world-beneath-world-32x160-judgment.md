# 32 × 160 scouting judgment

Date: 2026-10-11

## What was run

The scouting target is 32 seeded worlds × 160 weeks = 5,120 world-weeks. The first scouting engine was audited before accepting its output. Its thresholds were too inert: the earlier proxy rules could complete the horizon with essentially no irreversible proxy points. That is a model failure, not evidence that the fictional lives are uneventful.

A threshold-rich v2 was therefore added. It keeps all nominated moments explicitly `proxy_noncanonical`; selected moments still require replay through the full character/micro authority before they can become fiction. A local formula-equivalent execution of the 32 × 160 design was used for the structural judgment because this environment still cannot import the branch by cloning GitHub. The repository test suite is therefore not claimed as executed here.

## Audit result

The v2 run succeeds at one narrow task: it produces delayed, auditable pressure crossings instead of an empty history. Typical long arcs now include Chen Lan's housing transition and income-path pressure, later career reconsideration, followed by AI-driven role redefinition around Gu Wei and Zhao Ke. Delayed echoes are mechanically present after at least 26 weeks.

However, the 32 worlds remain much too similar. Most worlds nominate only about 4–6 proxy points, usually reaching three actors. The same Chen-Lan-centered early sequence appears repeatedly, and the Gu-Wei / Zhao-Ke AI-role crossings cluster late in the horizon. Random macro shocks alter timing more often than they alter the causal topology of a life.

The strongest structural scout in this batch is `world-022` (choice-pressure family): it reaches four actors and contains six proxy points, with Chen's housing and income pressures early, Gu Wei and Zhao Ke role redefinitions late, and late career reconsiderations for Chen and Lin. `world-025` is a useful mixed-family comparison because it also reaches four actors. Worlds 014, 020, 021 and 008 are useful timing controls for Chen's career reconsideration. These are candidates for diagnosis, not winners for a novel.

## Judgment

**The 32 × 160 experiment is finished, and the current architecture does not yet produce a masterpiece-scale world.**

This is a useful negative result. Macro → institution → actor pressure now works, but pressure crossing is still too close to a collection of independent gauges. A large fictional world needs endogenous social propagation: one person's choice must change another person's feasible set; institutions must remember prior decisions; family obligations must evolve; jobs, housing and collaboration must be allocated from scarce shared resources; relationships and reputations must carry path dependence; exits and arrivals must alter the network. Without those mechanisms, a macro shock changes several scores but rarely creates history.

The decisive missing object is therefore **endogenous consequence topology**, not more prose and not more random shocks.

## What should happen next

Do not spend model budget deeply replaying all eight candidates yet. Preserve `world-022` and `world-025` as regression cases, then add shared scarce resources, stateful institutional policy, evolving family obligations, network/reputation transmission, actor entry/exit and a restricted decision dispatcher. Re-run the same 32 × 160 benchmark. Promotion to expensive literary replay should require materially different causal graphs across seeds, at least one multi-actor consequence chain, at least one delayed consequence that changes another actor's feasible set, and no rule/knowledge/consent bypass.

The important conclusion from this batch is precise: **we can now generate pressure histories, but we cannot yet generate sufficiently entangled social histories.**
