# 2035 convergence replay pass 02

This replay continues the existing 2026–2035 candidate history. It applies pass-02 care/family/city mechanisms and enforces local information. Characters do not know which macro branch will win.

## Replay rule
At each decision, a character may use current income, savings, observed family health, current offers, known housing/care prices, current relationships and their own beliefs. They may not optimize against the author's 2035 endpoint.

## Chen Lan
2030: Chen uses authority she already has to fund a small paid training slot. She does not know whether budgets will tighten. The decision survives.

2031: a parent's health episode creates irregular coordination work. Community services remove routine tasks but cannot guarantee emergency coverage. Chen protects the existing trainee and freezes expansion. This survives both strong-care and weak-care branches; only the size of the freeze changes.

2032–33: she accumulates management reputation and cash, yet becomes more geographically sticky. In strong-care branches she explores outside offers; in weak-care branches she screens them out earlier. No branch permits the author to treat salary as a direct purchase of care freedom.

2034: delete the deterministic “Chen must reject promotion” event. Replace it with a fork. She rejects when contingency coverage is weak or family coordination cannot move; she accepts when trusted care plus household/sibling coverage is robust. The trunk consequence is identical at a higher level: promotion is priced against contingent care liability, not salary alone.

2035: if she stayed, she has less title growth but greater local authority; if she moved, she gains title and income but must spend money/political capital maintaining a care network. Neither branch is a moral reward.

## Lin Jing
2030–31: Lin keeps a geographically portable profile because she sees labor-market uncertainty. She cannot know which city will become favorable.

2032: delete the single-cause “fear makes Lin miss the move” as canonical. Preserve three local-information branches: she stays from status fear; she stays because a partner's job is less portable; she moves because a destination bundle (job + rent + family support) clears her threshold. What survives is option decay and bundle pricing.

2033: Xu refuses to endorse an unverifiable input. Lin experiences this as betrayal before she sees its longer institutional value. She reduces the claim rather than receiving retrospective wisdom. Relationship trust drops first; epistemic trust may recover later.

2034–35: in the partnership/child branch, a supposedly temporary reduction in mobility becomes sticky through lost network time and employer expectations. In the move branch, cheaper housing gives her time but a thinner professional network reduces outside options. In the stay branch, network density remains high while housing/care distance stays expensive. No branch yields a clean autonomy winner.

## Xu Hang
2030–32: verification work remains useful but incompletely priced. He keeps the failure archive because it reduces repeated mistakes locally, not because he knows it will become valuable in 2034.

2033: his refusal to waive evidence costs Lin time and damages friendship. Keep the damage. If the archive later becomes institutionally useful, that is a delayed consequence rather than proof that he was socially rewarded for virtue.

2034–35: in some branches the archive becomes training infrastructure; in others it stays an obscure internal practice. Delete any trunk claim that formal recognition must arrive. Retain: socially necessary work can remain underpriced for years, and trust can accumulate before compensation.

## Cross-character counterfactuals

### No dramatic technology shock
All three histories still move. Chen faces care/budget conversion; Lin faces city/relationship option pricing; Xu faces organizational valuation of reliability. Technology changes texture and occupation labels but is not required for the causal skeleton.

### Strong welfare/care improvement
The skeleton weakens in severity but survives. Chen gains feasible offers; Lin gains refusal power; hidden labor transfer becomes the goodness question. The story becomes less tragic without becoming causally empty.

### Regional boom outside Beijing
Lin's move probability rises. Chen may remain anchored. Xu's methods can travel through organizations even if he does not. This strengthens the distinction between portable expertise and portable life.

### Partnership/child shock
This is the strongest new attack. Lin's reversible-option capacity can fall sharply without a cash crisis. A temporary household bargain may become durable because career interruption is asymmetric. The trunk must therefore include household bargaining, not leave family in background texture.

### Housing recovery
A housing-price rebound changes wealth and rent/buy calculations but does not restore a single adulthood bundle. Owners gain balance-sheet relief; renters may face higher costs; geographic lock-in can strengthen. Main mechanism survives.

### Severe downturn
Hiring freezes and weak budgets increase conversion lags. Chen cannot institutionalize all good intentions; Lin hoards options; Xu's reliability work may be cut precisely when useful. This branch intensifies rather than creates the mechanism.

## Deletions after attack
1. Delete “Chen rejects the 2034 promotion” from the historical trunk. Keep it as a conditional event.
2. Delete “Lin loses mobility because of fear” from the trunk. Fear is one branch among household and city-bundle constraints.
3. Delete “Xu's archive receives formal recognition by 2035” as necessary history.
4. Delete any implication that expanded elder-care capacity mechanically releases equivalent family time.
5. Delete any ranking of cities as generic winners for young professionals.

## Stable 2026–2035 causal skeleton after deletion
1. Scarcity first appears administratively: limited training slots, jobs, housing security, attention and institutional slack.
2. People respond by accumulating different buffers: credentials, cash, networks, reliability, housing claims, reciprocal relationships.
3. Those buffers are only partially convertible because institutions reprice them at different speeds.
4. Care and family formation expose contingent-time liabilities and unequal reversibility that income alone cannot settle.
5. Migration becomes a bundle trade among opportunity density, housing/time, family support, welfare and future options.
6. Organizations discover some forms of socially useful work before compensation/status systems price them correctly.
7. Private compromises can harden into structural inequality when reversibility differs.
8. By 2035 no common scoreboard returns. Earlier private choices are repeatedly reinterpreted as exchange rates among money, time, mobility, care, competence, power and trust change.

## Truth / goodness / beauty gate
Truth: PASS for candidate trunk. It survives strong care, weak care, regional boom, housing recovery, severe downturn, partnership/child shock and a null technology-shock branch. Specific events were deleted where the evidence did not support inevitability.

Goodness: PASS with warning. The trunk does not equate independence with virtue and does not let care expansion erase the worker/spouse/sibling who bears residual cost. Good intentions can fail under budget constraints.

Beauty: PASS as structure, not prose. The strongest motif is changing exchange rates: what looked like success in 2027 can become immobility in 2034; what looked like pedantry in 2029 can become trust infrastructure later; a temporary family bargain can become a decade-long biography.

## Convergence judgment
Close, but do not declare final convergence in this pass. The major social mechanisms now survive the strongest non-technical attacks. One final pass should test whether additional high-quality evidence on retirement/social security, mental/physical health and climate/energy changes the causal skeleton or only local probabilities and texture. If it only changes texture, declare 2035 convergence and freeze the trunk until genuinely contradictory evidence appears.
