# 2035 convergence — pass 01

This pass does **not** declare convergence.

## What changed after extending the horizon

The 2031–2035 horizon attacks a weakness in the 2026–2030 trunk: early career success can look final when it is only the first adult settlement.

Three mechanisms strengthened:

1. **Care is a delayed causal operator.** A 2031 parent-health shock can reopen a 2028 career decision and change migration, housing and time without invalidating the earlier choice.
2. **Authority and resources separate.** Chen may become management yet lack budget to enact paid training. This makes goodness institutionally constrained rather than a personality reward.
3. **Value and price separate.** Xu can do socially important verification while remaining underpaid; he must preserve some problem-definition work to avoid occupational lock-in.

## Counterfactual attacks

### CF1 — no parent health shock
Lin keeps more geographic mobility. Her autonomy story becomes easier, but the core claim that autonomy depends on material/institutional supports remains. The care mechanism is important but not required for the whole trunk.

### CF2 — strong public/community care services
Care converts partly from family hours into taxes/insurance/cash/service coordination. Lin does not become "free"; the constraint changes form. This supports modeling welfare as option value.

### CF3 — prolonged weak demand
Chen's paid-training ideal shrinks. If the history requires the program to survive unchanged, it fails. If the history only requires her not to shift all screening cost onto juniors, it can survive at smaller scale. Keep the latter as trunk; keep program size conditional.

### CF4 — verification becomes highly paid
Xu's value/price gap narrows, but occupational lock-in can remain. Therefore "verification is underpaid" is conditional; "social value and price are distinct" is robust.

### CF5 — AI progress slows sharply
The main 2031–2035 mechanisms still operate: care, housing, welfare, demand, city switching, budget authority and training. This is evidence that the trunk is no longer secretly an AI story.

### CF6 — property recovery
Housing may regain wealth value, but liquidity, commute and care-proximity tradeoffs remain. Do not encode "housing always loses value"; encode heterogeneous housing functions.

## Truth / goodness / beauty review

### Truth
Improved. The new trunk avoids guaranteed 2035 outcomes and marks service, budget, junior and city-offer dependencies. Still missing exact household balance sheets and eligibility paths.

### Goodness
Improved. Chen's good intention can fail materially; Lin can buy care rather than perform purity; Xu can give juniors visible responsibility at a speed cost. No reward guarantee should be added.

### Beauty
Promising but not converged. Strong echoes now exist:
- 2026 scarce administrative window → 2034 scarce training budget;
- 2026 failed reproduction → 2035 giving a junior a real failed audit;
- 2027 income comparison → 2035 asking who has time;
- first graduation → second professional graduation → recognition that no success bundle ever fully reassembled.

Risk: these echoes can become too neat. Later replay must insert ordinary years and unrelated life texture so motifs are discovered, not announced.

## Current historical trunk after compression

Keep:
- the adult-success bundle becomes less reliable as one package;
- resources are partially convertible and conversion rates are institutionally determined;
- care and welfare change career freedom;
- organizational power and budget power differ;
- socially valuable work and market reward differ;
- moral choices have resource prerequisites and can fail without becoming hypocrisy;
- later life can reinterpret earlier choices without proving one life strategy universally correct.

Conditional:
- exact occupation, salary, city, housing tenure, marriage/children;
- scale of paid training;
- whether care is family-provided or service-provided;
- whether verification earns a wage premium;
- magnitude and timing of AI displacement.

Delete from any future canonical version:
- guaranteed "second graduation" triumph;
- guaranteed high-paid verification career;
- any single winner ranking in 2035.

## Why not converged yet

Need at least:
1. exact material replay for 2031–2035 (cash, hours, care, housing, benefits, eligibility);
2. character-specific information paths;
3. cross-scenario replay of the same choices under at least the six 2031–2035 envelopes;
4. more research on health/care delivery, migration/city divergence and household balance-sheet mechanisms;
5. ordinary-life density between turning points.

The next iteration should spend less effort inventing macro themes and more effort testing whether these mechanisms survive exact life accounting.
