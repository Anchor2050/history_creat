# Audit 2026–2030 causal continuity

Status: repair required before treating the pre-2030 years as a lived canon.

## Bottom line

The project does **not** lack a 2026–2030 plot spine. `trunk_replay_pass_01.py` already supplies eighteen causally ordered decisions from March 2026 through January 2030, and `history_capture-01.md` correctly turns them into five historical periods.

The missing layer is **biographical continuity**. The existing spine is strongest in work, AI repricing, verification, institutional ethics and Lin/Chen status comparison. Several resources that become decisive after 2030 are only described at half-year summary level before 2030: housing tenure, family coordination, health capacity, social-insurance continuity, geographic outside options, ordinary cash buffers and the maintenance cost of friendship.

This creates a handoff problem: the 2030–2035 trunk can appear to discover care, mobility and exercisable options too suddenly.

## What already exists and should be preserved

### 2026
- Chen uses an administrative exception, graduates and enters an external research-service job.
- Lin pays an indirect scarcity cost and then makes a bad autonomy-as-isolation choice.
- A deliberately boring interval is preserved.

### 2027
- Chen's material rise enters the friendship as an income/housing comparison wound.
- She apologizes without erasing the wound.
- Time poverty begins and retraining costs a promotion cycle.
- Lin begins repricing autonomy away from technical virtuosity.

### 2028
- Chen owns a client-side failure.
- Lin refuses a higher-paid role dominated by invisible verification labor.
- Xu receives unattractive failure-audit work and later converts it into a verification-oriented job opportunity.

### 2029–January 2030
- Xu imposes a provenance cost on Lin's project.
- Chen protects paid trial/training instead of pushing screening cost onto juniors.
- Xu's old failures become training material.
- Lin and Chen stop answering the old one-dimensional question of who is 'doing best'.

These events remain valuable. The repair should not replace them merely to make the prequel look new.

## Missing causal tissue

### 1. Housing is thematic but not yet a ledger
We know housing matters, but before 2030 we rarely know who renewed a lease, paid a deposit, accepted a commute, declined a purchase, received family housing pressure, or lost/gained mobility through tenure. This weakens the causal basis of Lin's 2030 decision to renew rather than buy.

Repair requirement: at least two ordinary housing decisions before 2030 must leave persistent state, without making renting morally superior.

### 2. Family obligation enters too abstractly
Chen's higher income is said to create family claims, but the pre-2030 replay has no concrete, bounded family coordination history. Her father's 2031 surgery therefore risks feeling like care was introduced because the later plot needed it.

Repair requirement: establish ordinary pre-illness family coordination — transfers, retirement timing, appointments, holiday logistics, sibling/kin coverage if applicable — while keeping the 2031 health shock genuinely unforeseeable.

### 3. Mobility is treated as personality before it becomes an exercised option
Lin is called more autonomous, but her geographic outside options are not continuously maintained in the old replay. The 2030–2035 trunk correctly defines freedom as exercisable option capacity; the earlier years must show the maintenance work behind that capacity.

Repair requirement: at least one plausible external-city/institution inquiry that Lin does not have to accept, plus the mundane costs of keeping documents, networks, lease timing and benefit continuity portable.

### 4. Social insurance and welfare are nearly invisible
Later history treats pension, medical insurance and welfare eligibility as conversion infrastructure. Before 2030, employment transitions occur without recording whether benefits are continuous or whether changing institution/city would disrupt them.

Repair requirement: record benefit continuity as a practical constraint at transitions; do not turn policy into exposition.

### 5. Health exists mainly as future shock
The later trunk tracks physical/mental capacity, but the first five years implicitly assume full capacity except for workload stress.

Repair requirement: introduce ordinary capacity variation — sleep, recovery, routine health visits, overwork limits — without inventing dramatic diagnoses to create plot.

### 6. Xu has a professional biography but a thin ordinary life
Xu's verification arc is causally strong, yet housing, money, friendship and location barely change. This risks making him a function of the theme.

Repair requirement: give Xu a material reason for accepting/narrowing into verification work and record one cost that is neither prestige nor publication count.

### 7. Relationship maintenance is event-based rather than time-based
Lin and Chen wound and repair the friendship, but fewer meetings, cancelled plans and changed spending norms are summarized rather than lived. Lin/Xu trust similarly jumps from professional disagreement to later epistemic trust.

Repair requirement: preserve low-drama contacts and missed contacts so trust changes through repetition, not only set-piece conversations.

### 8. The 2030 handoff is implicit
Post-2030 events cite `r12`, `r16`, `r18` and other earlier nodes, but there is no single state handoff showing what each character actually carries into 2030 across money, housing, time, health, care, mobility, status, bargaining power, benefits and trust.

Repair requirement: construct an explicit 2030 state vector with provenance to prior events.

## Repair principles

1. Preserve the existing eighteen decisions unless a counterfactual proves one unnecessary or inconsistent.
2. Add connective events only when they change state or explain why a later option is feasible.
3. Keep at least one long interval in which nothing dramatic happens but money, leases, skills, fatigue or relationships drift.
4. Do not seed the exact 2031 illness in advance. Seed care infrastructure and coordination habits, not prophecy.
5. Do not make Lin's mobility a virtue. Maintaining an outside option costs money, attention and relational commitment.
6. Do not make Chen's income automatically buy freedom. Record where cash buys relief and where it creates claims.
7. Do not make Xu's verification specialty guaranteed foresight. He accepts it under constrained alternatives and pays opportunity costs.
8. Make the final 2030 state compatible with more than one 2030–2035 branch.

## Completion criterion

The repair is complete when a reader can stop at January 2030 and answer, without using future knowledge:
- what each character can afford;
- where and how securely they live;
- how much unowned time they have;
- what family obligations are already normal;
- which moves/jobs they can realistically refuse;
- what benefit or location frictions constrain those moves;
- who trusts whom and why;
- which apparently sensible adaptations may later become liabilities.

Only then should 2030–2035 feel like history continuing rather than a second model starting.
