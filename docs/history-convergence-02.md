## Important correction

The deepest robust history is not 'AI makes execution cheap and responsibility expensive.' That remains a powerful conditional mechanism.

The more stable history is:

> **From 2026 into the early 2030s, the old package of adulthood begins to come apart. Degree, stable employment, home ownership, marriage/family formation, city residence and professional status no longer move together reliably. People gain autonomy in some dimensions while becoming newly dependent in others. The decisive inequality is increasingly not just income, but who has enough money, welfare, family capacity, city access and institutional bargaining power to keep options open.**

Technology intensifies this separation; property adjustment, aging/care, welfare reform, urban restructuring, regional divergence and external volatility can produce it even when AI diffusion is slower than expected.

## Character consequences

### Chen Lan

Her durable arc is not guaranteed upward mobility. Her durable problem is conversion: can a late doctoral graduate convert education into material security without converting all new income into family obligation and all new authority into responsibility for organizational transition?

In favorable futures she completes the 'second graduation' and protects paid training. In weak-demand futures she may acquire judgment without acquiring enough budget to act on her ethics. That branch is more historically honest and should remain alive.

### Lin Jing

Lin becomes the strongest carrier of the robust spine. Her central resource is option value: the ability to refuse a role, city, claim, housing commitment or status comparison. But autonomy is materially produced. Parent care, rent, benefits, health and institutional position can remove it.

Her story must therefore test the price of refusal rather than celebrate refusal abstractly.

### Xu Hang

Xu's verification arc remains valuable but conditional. A society can need verification while refusing to pay young people well for it. He may become indispensable and still be under-rewarded. This is stronger than guaranteed vindication.

## The history we are beginning to catch

The emerging object is a **decomposition of the old life bundle**.

Earlier social expectations linked:

education -> stable occupation -> income -> housing -> marriage/family -> local belonging -> retirement security.

The 2026-2030 world increasingly permits mismatched combinations:

- high income + low time sovereignty;
- prestigious degree + weak job security;
- rented housing + high autonomy;
- home ownership + geographic immobility;
- valuable expertise + weak bargaining power;
- management authority + insufficient budget to be humane;
- family closeness + heavy care constraint;
- social protection + greater willingness to change jobs;
- technical abundance + scarcity of trusted responsibility.

That decomposition should now drive the simulation.

## Convergence decision

We have **partial convergence**, not final convergence.

Freeze as historical spine:
1. adulthood-success dimensions separate rather than move together;
2. option value/autonomy is materially produced and unequally distributed;
3. housing becomes one constraint/asset among several, not a universal ending;
4. a single 'winner' ranking becomes increasingly misleading.

Keep conditional:
- Chen's clean material ascent;
- AI-driven second graduation;
- Xu's market reward for verification;
- successful institutionalization of costly goodness.

Next iteration should attack the frozen spine with household-care, health, city and external-shock counterfactuals. If those mainly change texture and branch outcomes but not the four frozen claims, convergence is close. If they break the spine, research must reopen it.
