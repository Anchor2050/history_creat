# Canon trunk 2030–2035 v1

Status: **FROZEN AFTER STRUCTURAL CONVERGENCE**

This document freezes mechanisms, not scenes.

## Historical engine
The period is organized by **institutional asynchrony**. Labor markets, housing, welfare, care systems, retirement rules, health capacity, family obligations and systems of professional recognition change at different speeds. Characters therefore live inside conversion lags: a resource that was valuable in one institution or year may convert badly into another.

## Human state vector
For each major character track at least:
- income and liquid buffer;
- housing security and location lock-in;
- usable time and variance of time demands;
- physical/mental capacity;
- care obligations and coordination robustness;
- mobility;
- status and institutional recognition;
- bargaining/refusal power;
- welfare/social-insurance eligibility;
- exercisable reversible-option capacity.

No scalar 'success score' is permitted.

## Character trunk

### Lin Jing
Her apparent advantage is mobility and preserved optionality. The engine must periodically test whether those options remain exercisable. Fear, health, family ties, city bundles and simple non-use can decay them. Her story cannot resolve into 'freedom wins'.

### Chen Lan
Her apparent advantage is institutional authority and relatively legible career progress. Care and coordination expose the difference between income and usable time. Her attempt to make training labor paid remains morally important precisely because budgets can stop it. Her story cannot resolve into 'responsibility is rewarded'.

### Xu Hang
His failure archive converts private embarrassment into potentially useful institutional memory. He must retain the right to refuse evidentiary shortcuts even when refusal harms a friend. Recognition may arrive late, partially, or when he lacks the capacity to capitalize on it. His story cannot resolve into 'integrity earns success'.

## 2030–2035 pressure sequence
2030 — preserved options are still visible; attempts are made to convert authority and experience into durable institutions.

2031 — care, budgets and coordination attack usable freedom. Income proves only partially convertible into time.

2032 — an owned option is allowed to decay or become less exercisable. Earlier status wounds return in altered form.

2033 — friendship and evidentiary standards collide. A smaller verifiable claim survives at real personal cost.

2034 — accumulated failures become useful across an institutional boundary while family/care constraints reprice career and mobility.

2035 — recognition, if it comes, lags behind need. No character receives a final settlement. What survives is a changed ledger of conversions, obligations and usable options.

## Truth constraints
Macro forces must enter through mechanisms that touch daily life: hiring, pay, budgets, rent/mortgages, welfare eligibility, pension contribution history, clinic access, health capacity, commuting, care schedules, infrastructure failure, insurance and family coordination.

Do not use trend exposition as causation.

## Goodness constraints
Good actions may fail. Refusal may hurt people. Care may create resentment. Institutions can make moral action affordable or unaffordable. Never guarantee reward for virtue.

## Beauty constraints
Beauty should emerge from recurrence with changed meaning. Preferred echoes include:
- administrative scarcity -> refusal to externalize costs -> budget ceiling -> accumulated failure becoming teaching material;
- income/status wound -> later reversed envy -> discovery that the compared resources were never equivalent;
- an option preserved -> option unused -> option impaired -> recognition that freedom has maintenance costs.

Do not manufacture symmetry merely to close an arc.

## Generation rule
From this trunk, generate competing concrete histories. Every candidate history must pass:
1. character causality;
2. institutional feasibility;
3. resource-accounting consistency;
4. counterfactual plausibility;
5. truth/goodness/beauty review.

Selection may favor tension and aesthetic power only among histories that pass all five gates.

## Reopening rule
Reopen this trunk only if new evidence or simulation demonstrates that one of its causal mechanisms fails. New occupations, technologies, policy details, illnesses, romances, disasters or city-specific facts normally update state and branch probabilities without changing the trunk.
