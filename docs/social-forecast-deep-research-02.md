# Social forecast deep research 02 — world before plot

Status: research-grounded forecast design, 2026-2030. Observed anchors and modeled futures are kept separate.

## What changed in this pass

The earlier trunk leaned too heavily on AI/task repricing. This pass treats technology as one force inside a larger transition involving demand, property, welfare, aging, family, health, urban form, regional mobility, climate and external fragmentation.

## Observed anchors as of 2026

### Demand, property and household security

IMF and World Bank 2026 assessments both describe resilient growth alongside weak/cautious domestic demand and continued property adjustment. Both emphasize stronger social protection as part of a consumption rebalancing. This means a young professional's salary cannot be translated directly into felt security: housing expectations, parental buffers, benefits and future-care liabilities affect saving and risk-taking.

### Employment and social protection

The 15th Five-Year employment plan explicitly treats structural mismatch, graduate/youth employment and the employment effects of new technology as policy problems. The broader plan expands protection for flexible/new-form workers and emphasizes portability. World generation therefore separates gross salary from benefit quality, unemployment protection, occupational injury coverage and portability.

### Aging, fertility and care

The 15th Five-Year plan expands long-term care, community/home eldercare, childcare and birth-support systems. It also calls for shared parenting responsibility. The model does not assume these policies restore fertility. Instead, care capacity becomes a household resource whose public/private allocation changes careers and migration.

### Health and mental health

The national health plan expands life-cycle care, occupational health, mental-health services and climate-health adaptation. Health is therefore not only a catastrophic random shock. Chronic strain, psychological support access, reproductive health and parent health can gradually alter time budgets.

### Housing and urban form

The urban-renewal plan shifts emphasis toward existing-city renewal, rental markets,保障 housing, youth/new-resident housing difficulty, complete communities and resilient infrastructure. Home ownership remains culturally and materially important but cannot be used as a universal success scalar.

### Regional mobility

The national plan emphasizes metropolitan integration and public-service equalization by residence. The world therefore allows careers to be solved spatially: core-city job + peripheral residence, provincial-capital move, return migration, or cross-city household arrangements.

### External fragmentation

IMF analysis reports much faster US-China trade decoupling after the 2025 escalation than in 2018-19, while Chinese intermediate-goods exports are increasingly embedded in other regional supply chains. This creates firm-level volatility without requiring a single collapse scenario. Client demand, hiring and budgets may be rerouted across sectors and cities.

### Climate

Urban and health plans normalize climate adaptation and resilient infrastructure. Heat and flood enter through commute, eldercare, health, housing quality and work interruption. They should rarely appear as speeches about climate.

## Five correlated futures

We now use five correlated futures instead of independently randomizing every domain:

1. **slow_rebalance (0.32 prior)** — modest demand improvement, incremental welfare strengthening, continued adjustment;
2. **long_adjustment (0.23)** — weak demand/property drag persists, hiring and mobility stay cautious;
3. **social_dividend (0.17)** — productivity gains combine with stronger welfare/care/public services;
4. **unequal_productivity (0.18)** — output/productivity rise faster than worker security and bargaining power;
5. **fragmented_shock (0.10)** — external fragmentation plus weak demand constrains firms and households.

These priors are engineering assumptions for stress tests. They are not probabilities claimed by any source.

## New world rule

No character receives a macro variable directly. Every force must transmit through household, city, university, firm, family or relationship state.

Examples:
- weak demand -> client budgets -> firm training budget -> Chen's authority to protect junior training;
- aging -> parent health/care hours -> migration constraint -> Lin's feasible job set;
- urban renewal -> rental/public-service quality -> commute/care feasibility -> buy/rent/stay choice;
- trade fragmentation -> client volatility -> hiring freeze -> Xu's verification niche may disappear even if epistemically valuable;
- stronger social protection -> lower downside of job change -> greater practical autonomy;
- heat/flood -> commute and eldercare disruption -> lost work time -> relationship bargaining.

## Anti-prophecy rule

A forecast is allowed to determine the distribution of worlds, never the destiny of a person. If a character arc survives only one favorable future, it is a conditional branch. Only cross-scenario mechanisms may enter the historical spine.
